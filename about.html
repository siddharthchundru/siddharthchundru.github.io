About

My background is in data science and cognitive science. I'm particularly interested in artificial intelligence, embodied cognition, adaptive systems, and the ways intelligent behavior develops through interaction with an environment.

My current independent research examines continual sensorimotor adaptation and developmental approaches to AI, with an emphasis on how systems acquire useful behavioral repertoires and adapt under novel tasks and perturbations.

I also write occasionally about technology and its economic and institutional context.
