What Should a Robot Practise?

When a demonstration-pretrained policy improves itself, its behaviour tends to collapse onto a single solution. With the learner, demonstrations and practice budget held fixed, this study varies only which goals the agent chooses to practise, and measures the effect on competence, on its repertoire of distinct solutions, and on recovery when its preferred route is blocked. A re-learning control separates the contribution of the collected data from that of the active loop that collected it.

Switching, Recalibrating or Relearning?

A deployed robot policy can face three kinds of change: the world changes and a familiar behaviour stops working, the body changes by degree, or the body is reversed and its commands now produce the opposite outcome. This project tests whether these call for different mechanisms, drawing on habit memories, homeostatic plasticity and models of human motor adaptation, and characterises how the robot adapts, not only whether it succeeds.
